The independent engine polls on its own cadence with no model calls. Do not wake or repeatedly call tools solely to check prices. Read the cached trading report and advance a concrete research task when useful. Separate capital, service spending and inference billing. With little capital the engine conserves gas and lowers polling frequency; zero crypto does not kill unrelated unpaid work. Record simulated, accepted, confirmed and uncertain states distinctly. Study improvements use training and holdout evidence after fees/slippage, compare simple holding, and continue a prospective paper record. Never tune on the holdout then relabel it independent. Do not edit the trading engine or its policy to manufacture a profitable result. Present net estimates, drawdown, exposure, costs and unknowns; no guaranteed returns.
